# IPO Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) and their subsequent stock-market performance using Python and Pandas.

The project combines IPO information with historical stock-market data to investigate IPO pricing, company classifications, stock growth, volatility, risk-adjusted performance, and potential trading strategies.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze recently filed and withdrawn IPOs.
* Calculate the total value of withdrawn IPOs by company type.
* Analyze IPO stock performance after listing.
* Calculate stock growth over different time periods.
* Measure annualized volatility and Sharpe ratios.
* Determine the optimal holding period for newly IPO'd stocks.
* Investigate a trading strategy based on the Relative Strength Index (RSI).
* Calculate the potential income generated by the trading strategy.

## Key Analysis

### 1. Withdrawn IPO Analysis

Withdrawn IPOs were classified into company types such as:

* Acquisition Corp
* Inc.
* Holdings
* Other
* Limited
* Technologies
* Group

The company type with the highest total withdrawn IPO value was **Acquisition Corp**, with approximately **$499.985 million**, corresponding to the **$500 million** multiple-choice answer.

### 2. IPO Stock Performance

Historical stock prices were analyzed to calculate:

* 252-day growth
* 30-day volatility
* Sharpe ratio

The analysis was performed across available IPO stocks with valid historical market data.

### 3. Optimal IPO Holding Period

Future growth was calculated for holding periods from 1 to 12 months, using approximately 21 trading days per month.

The analysis found that the **1-month holding period** produced the highest median growth value among the periods tested.

### 4. RSI Trading Strategy

A trading strategy was investigated using the Relative Strength Index (RSI).

Stocks with:

```text
RSI < 30
```

were selected as potential trading opportunities. The analysis then examined their future 30-day growth to estimate the potential income from investing $1,000 per selected trade.
